Turn promising agents into observable, controllable services with explicit permissions, governed memory, evaluation, human oversight and execution evidence. Blockchain & Verifiable Computing

Use signatures, attestations, shared state, smart contracts or ZK only when another party must establish a bounded property independently. A 2026 matched prototype found the database-backed endpoint materially faster than the ledger-backed endpoint on raw throughput, which is negative evidence against treating DLT as a generic performance upgrade.

Database vs ledger throughput · bounded prototype
Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience · 2026 study context
MetricDatabase-backed endpoint: 924.79 tx/s; ledger-backed endpoint: 219.23 tx/s.WorkloadSame 1,000 TPS target workload in a local two-organization SSO/TOTP prototype.
Boundary: one bounded prototype. It does not establish a universal DB-vs-DLT multiplier and does not measure reconciliation or administrative coordination.

Where programmable shared-state coordination is the property, ECB describes automating collateral substitution, margin management and collateral return across institutions; that is coordination evidence, not a throughput claim.
