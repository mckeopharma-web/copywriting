What breaks

The defect is not “too little documentation.” It is a broken link between risk, change and evidence.

Uniform validation

Every function gets the same treatment regardless of product, patient, data or process consequence. ICH Q9(R1) frames quality-risk management as applicable across pharmaceutical quality activities from development and manufacturing through distribution, inspection and regulatory review.

Evidence assembled after the event

Source, version, actor, test result and approval cannot be reconstructed from the system history.

Decision rights stay implicit

Exceptions and return-to-service decisions are remembered socially rather than represented as controlled, reviewable state.

Method before tools

Risk → requirement → test → evidence → authorisation.

Build one inspectable decision chain instead of treating validation as an end-of-project document pack.

For computerised systems used in GMP-regulated activities, Annex 11 ties validation extent and data-integrity controls to justified, documented risk assessment and requires lifecycle traceability of user requirements.

In a different scope, FDA’s February 2026 final Computer Software Assurance guidance also describes risk-based assurance for medical-device production and QMS software, applying additional rigor where risk warrants it.

FAQ

Questions that change scope or accountability.

Do you sign validation documents?

No. I can structure and evidence the package; authorised organisational roles retain accountability.

Can an AI workflow fall inside validation scope?

It depends on intended use, GxP context, data, consequence, authority, change, performance criteria and oversight. Phase 1 bounds these before defining assurance.

Do you represent us during an inspection?

Not as statutory representative. The work prepares evidence, deviations, owners and rationale with your teams.

Must the whole system be revalidated?

No universal answer is assumed. Phase 1 creates a documented risk and lifecycle rationale for the bounded assurance scope.
