The product

The product is not a deck. It is a versioned marketing decision system.

Answer four questions fast: what do we know, what are we testing, what did we observe, what changes next?

Source→Context→Hypothesis→Test→Observation→Decision

Context registry

Avatars, offers, objections, sources, approved claims, assumptions and gaps under version control.

Experiment register

Each material message gets a metric, population, period, comparator and decision rule.

Prompt / automation contract

Generation uses governed context; missing evidence routes to refusal or review.

FAQ

What usually blocks the decision.

Short answers; proof one layer deeper.

Is this a marketing agency?

No. It builds the context, measurement and decision system around execution.

Do you produce content?

Reference content can prove the system; continuous production stays with the team or agency.

Do we need a new CRM?

No by default. Existing tools remain unless replacement is independently justified.

Can you guarantee revenue growth?

No. Deliverables and acceptance criteria can be bounded; market outcomes cannot.

What happens to unsupported claims?

They are narrowed, marked as hypotheses, queued for validation or removed from governed generation.

What happens to failed tests?

They remain in the evidence register so they are not recycled as “new ideas.”
