Proposition

Turn governance into runtime controls, not policy text.

The mechanism is a production-control architecture: identity and authority outside prompt intent; governed memory; tool boundaries; versioned evaluation; reconstructable execution evidence; explicit human promotion and rollback.

Before → after

From scattered pilots to a governed promotion system.

Before

Several vendors and prototypes; broad governance policies; permissions fragmented across tools; prompt and memory versions difficult to reconstruct; production confidence largely anecdotal.

After

Named agentic-system owners; enumerated authority; governed memory; versioned evaluations; execution evidence; explicit promotion and rollback gates.

The after-state is not “a smarter agent.” It is an organisation able to answer four operating questions without reconstructing them in a meeting: what may it do, what does it know, how is it tested, and how is it stopped?
