Triggers

The problem starts when agentic AI crosses a decision boundary.

These are operating moments, not generic “AI maturity” symptoms.

01 · A pilot needs promotion

The workflow can now change external state, but no one can enumerate the authority it should receive.

02 · Governance must become executable

Policies exist, while runtime permissions, memory rules and release controls still live in prompts, documents or manual review.

03 · An incident must be reconstructed

The team has logs, but not a decision record linking objective, context, tools, sources, policy and human intervention.

04 · A model or tool must change

The organisation needs regression evidence before it can defend a production promotion or rollback.

Consequences

Once agents use tools and memory, the evaluation surface expands beyond model quality.
