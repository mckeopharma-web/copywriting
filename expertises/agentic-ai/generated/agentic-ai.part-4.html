Evidence before promotion

Release criteria are defined before a new model, prompt, tool or policy is promoted.

FAQ

The objections a platform buyer should resolve before procurement.

“We already have a consultancy.”Keep it. This mandate is useful where strategy must become an inspectable technical boundary: authority, evals, runtime evidence and promotion gates.
“Our data team can do it.”Yes—if it can own the authority model, evaluation lifecycle, observability and handoff. The engagement is designed to leave those assets with the internal team.
“Governance will slow innovation.”The target is not blanket approval. It is to make the smallest consequential controls executable so routine promotion can become more repeatable.
“We are not mature enough.”Maturity is not the entry criterion. A named consequential workflow, evidence access and a real production decision are.

Engagement

Bring one consequential workflow, one accountable owner and one production decision.

First output: a bounded decision brief—what may change, who owns it, which authority is required, how it will be evaluated, and what makes promotion or rollback defensible.
