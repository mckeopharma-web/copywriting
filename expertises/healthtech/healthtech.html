CDM
Clinical data leadership

Clinical Data Management Automation

Reduce manual query, reconciliation and export friction while preserving the lineage needed to reconstruct a lock decision.

Trigger: query/reconciliation/lock pressure. Before → after: semi-manual transformations → versioned QC, exception queues and explicit human lock decision. Driver: speed without losing reconstructability.

Scope: data-flow automation, versioned controls, transformation lineage, exceptions and review evidence. No claim that automation itself improves clinical outcomes.

PV
Pharmacovigilance leadership

Pharmacovigilance Data Engineering

Automate evidence ingestion, extraction and prioritisation while keeping analysis, rationale, escalation and safety judgement accountable to the PV process.

Trigger: signal/case workload consumes the team. Before → after: manual monitoring → supervised queues with source/version lineage and explicit human safety authority. Driver: throughput without delegating causality or safety decisions.

Automation can prepare and prioritise evidence; it does not establish that a medicine caused an event or replace the accountable PV decision.

HT
HealthTech / PharmaTech product leadership

HealthTech & PharmaTech Product Engineering

Turn pharma requirements into increments where intended use, risk, permissions, acceptance criteria, validation evidence and human review are designed with the feature—not added at handover.

Trigger: evidence and regulatory documentation lag delivery. Before → after: late reconstruction → bounded acceptance evidence and review ownership in the backlog. Driver: verifiable sprint-by-sprint delivery.
