APA Programme · bounded 12-week mandate

Turn one opaque AI prototype into a production decision your CTO, security lead and governance owner can defend.

One critical use case. Twelve weeks. A decision package for GO, LIMITED GO or NO-GO — with explicit authority, production tests, execution evidence and a handover your team can operate.

01 · Triggers

Recognise one of these moments — or the programme is premature.

The trigger is not “we want AI.” It is a concrete production decision with a known owner, date and consequence.

01

Demo → production date

The prototype works in a controlled demo; somebody now has to own the production boundary.

02

“Show me the evidence”

Leadership asks what the release decision rests on. The evidence package does not yet exist.

03

Security arrives late

Security or compliance sees the system at the end and discovers permissions, data flows or failure modes it never approved.

04

Pilot incident, weak reconstruction

The team can describe what happened but cannot reconstruct model, memory, tool calls, controls and human decision as one trace.

05

Second use case, first still ungoverned

Scaling begins before the first production pattern is bounded and reusable.

06

Key builder leaves

The system works because a person remembers how it works. The operating model is not transferable.

02 · Consequences

A successful prompt is not a production acceptance test.

For tool-using agents, the relevant surface includes tasks, tools and untrusted data — not only model outputs.

Evaluation must include adversarial cases

AgentDojo is a peer-reviewed benchmark with 97 realistic tasks and 629 security test cases for tool-using LLM agents over untrusted data.

This supports scenario breadth. It does not estimate your production attack rate.

Production questions are architectural

  • Who owns the action?
  • Which tools and data can it reach?
  • What evidence is retained?
  • What blocks promotion?
  • Who can stop or roll back?
  • Can the team replay the decision?

04 · Qualification

The mandate only works when the evidence can actually be reached.

Qualification protects both sides from a twelve-week engagement that cannot produce a defensible work product.

Fit

  • A real promotion, audit, launch, renewal or replacement decision exists.
  • A named sponsor can arbitrate scope and accept the work product.
  • Systems, data, logs or documents can be accessed under NDA during the first three weeks.
  • One critical use case can be bounded.

No-fit

  • Trend panorama or generic AI inspiration.
  • A statutory signature that only your authorised role can make.
  • No access to any evidence.
  • Generic prompt training without a production use case.
  • Enterprise-wide transformation without sponsor and bounded scope.
05 · Proposition

Build the decision system before asking the committee for a decision.

The mechanism is the Production Readiness Evidence Loop™: Frame → Design → Control → Test → Transfer. It creates a productised decision path, not a generic governance layer.

NCSC’s August 2026 interim agentic-AI guidance is organised around seven operational considerations, including threat modelling, oversight, sandboxing, observability, attribution and emergency shutdown.

The programme does not claim to be the NCSC method. The guidance supports the architecture question: production assurance must cover the operating environment around the model.

09 · Results

What success means — and what is not claimed.

Success is an acceptance state of the work product, not a fabricated client KPI.

Acceptance outcomes

  • The use case reaches the readiness level agreed in phase 1, with evidence.
  • The internal team executes the loop once without external intervention.
  • Known exceptions and residual risks are documented and owned.

Explicit non-claims

  • No guarantee that the system receives GO.
  • No guarantee of regulatory approval, certification or inspection outcome.
  • No public client outcome metrics are simulated.
  • No universal ROI claim is inferred from research benchmarks.

12 · Process

Five phases. Each phase produces something reviewable.

NIST’s 2026 TEVV-Athlon draft uses a four-stage framework for context-specific AI assessment and explicitly includes agentic systems in scope. The programme below is a separate first-party delivery sequence, not a claim of NIST conformance.

Weeks 1–2 · Frame

Use case, baseline, risk map, owners, constraints and acceptance criteria.

Weeks 3–4 · Design

Target architecture, data boundaries, permissions, instrumentation, evaluation and promotion plan.

Weeks 5–9 · Implement

Quality gates, tests, observability, execution evidence, review workflow and integrations.

Weeks 10–11 · Test

Functional scenarios, regressions, security, simulated incidents, exceptions and readiness decision package.

Week 12 · Transfer

Runbooks, documentation, responsible-owner training and one loop executed by the internal team.

Why “human approval” is not the whole design

A recent preprint shows that external authorization can materially change benchmark outcomes under a defined protocol: AgentDojo exfiltration fell from 75–100% to 0% across four reported domains in the paper’s compromised-model evaluation.

This is protocol-bounded research evidence. The programme does not promise the same security result.

16 · Questions

Objections are design inputs.

Each objection maps to a purchase driver in the Customer Avatar rather than to generic reassurance.

Why not leave this to our internal data team?

If your team already owns permissions, evaluation, observability, release evidence and transfer, remove those modules from scope. The programme exists for the decision gap between a working prototype and an accountable production operating model.

Won’t governance slow innovation?

The design target is to move controls into the delivery path: criteria known before promotion, automated checks where deterministic, and explicit exceptions where judgement is required.

We already use ChatGPT / a model API. Isn’t that enough?

A model endpoint is one dependency. Production assurance covers the surrounding system: data reach, tools, identity, memory, evaluation, logs, escalation and ownership.

How is ROI handled?

Baseline the variables your organisation can actually observe — cycle time, manual review effort, quality failures, tool cost, incidents or rework — before implementation. No ROI percentage is published here without a matched client baseline.

Do you guarantee compliance?

No. The mandate produces controls, evidence and recommendations that can support a compliance process. Legal classification, certification and statutory sign-off remain with competent roles.

What remains after you leave?

Architecture, source-controlled implementation, evaluation assets, runbooks, evidence records, backlog, decision boundaries and named internal owners.
