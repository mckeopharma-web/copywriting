Provenance policy

Creation, contribution, proof and confidence level.

A trust decision needs two boundaries kept separate: what the engineer demonstrably implemented, and when a blockchain mechanism is actually justified. Repository ownership alone is not authorship evidence.

Across these two records: 30 attributed commits out of 33 total, a deterministic aggregate of 26/29 and 4/4—not sole authorship, code quality or production impact.

ECB describes programmability as allowing settlement conditions to span an instrument lifecycle and explicitly notes that neither atomicity nor programmability is unique to tokenisation. That is the right architecture constraint: mechanism follows the verification and coordination requirement, not the technology label.

ECB also frames an integrated tokenised ecosystem around five capability categories—interoperable, transferable, portable, controllable and programmable. Here they are used as requirements vocabulary, not as proof that these projects implement an institutional market architecture.
