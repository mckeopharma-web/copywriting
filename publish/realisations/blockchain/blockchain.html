Provenance policy

Creation, contribution, proof and confidence level.

Repository ownership alone is not authorship evidence. The public record combines commit attribution, code scope, project context and explicit boundaries.

Across these two records: 30 attributed commits out of 33 total, a deterministic aggregate of 26/29 and 4/4—not sole authorship.

For buyer relevance, programmability is treated as a coordination mechanism when shared rules and conditional execution matter. ECB describes programmable repo workflows automating collateral substitution, margin management and return across institutions; this is not evidence every workflow needs DLT.

ECB also frames an integrated tokenised ecosystem around five capabilities—interoperable, transferable, portable, controllable and programmable—useful as a requirements checklist rather than a chain-selection slogan.
