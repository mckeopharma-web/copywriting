Frustrations & fears

What pushes this buyer away from the status quo.

These are role-model tensions, not attributed customer quotes.

01

Release velocity collides with assurance

Security evidence arrives as a late reconstruction instead of part of the release path.

02

Agent authority widens the control surface

Tools, credentials, data access and side effects change what “safe enough” means.

03

Audit evidence is repeatedly rebuilt

Each audit, renewal or customer request becomes another evidence-collection project.

04

Applicable obligations are time-bound

Where legal requirements apply, missing scope or evidence can no longer be treated as a maturity exercise.

On 4 August 2026, the UK NCSC called for strong safeguards, real-time oversight and clear response plans from the outset, and said post-incident detection alone is insufficient.

Consequences

The object to control is authority, not only model output.

Prompting is not the control plane

Source code is not the whole surface

Network reach, identities, tools, connectors, memory and reachable data also shape exposure.

Human approval is not self-validating

The approval context and the consequence of the approved action remain part of the risk model.

SYNTHESIS · NOT CUSTOMER VERBATIM

“La question n’est pas seulement de savoir si le modèle est sûr ; c’est ce que le système est autorisé à faire quand il se trompe.”

Use this as a writing lens until a real verbatim replaces it. The European Commission’s current AI Act overview distinguishes four risk levels for AI systems. Applicability of specific obligations remains system- and timeline-dependent.

Key purchase drivers

What must be true before this buyer can defend a yes.

Must-haves

✓ Enforceable control boundary.
✓ Named owner and residual-risk path.
✓ Evidence bound to the release artifact.
✓ Explicit exception and rollback criteria.
✓ Integration with the existing delivery chain.

Service expectation

Assurance + implementation + handoff. The consultant can design controls, gates and evidence; the work does not replace the client’s accountable authority, regulator, auditor or certification body.

Known deal-killers

✕ No accountable sponsor.
✕ No access to the evidence boundary.
✕ Expectation of a certification guarantee.
✕ Report-only engagement where enforcement is the real requirement.

Mechanism

Risk → Policy → Test → Evidence → Promote / Block.

The commercial mechanism is deliberately narrower than a promise of “safer AI”: translate a bounded risk statement into controls the delivery chain can enforce and a release record another accountable person can inspect later.

Process

Four phases. Four usable stopping points.

Surface

Inventory agents, connectors, credentials and reachable data. Output: boundary + threat model.

Gate

Translate controls into blocking conditions. Output: live release gate.

Evidence

Bind promotion to policy, results, exceptions, artifact identity and approver.

Handoff

The team adds a control and executes an exception path without the consultant.

Control blocks

Select modules from the risk boundary, not from a fixed checklist.

Threat modelling

Objective, action surface, reachable services, prompts/context, failure scenarios and red lines.

Agent isolation & authority

Capability boundaries, network restrictions, credential scope, escalation and external authorization where justified.

Supply chain & promotion

SBOM, dependencies, images, secrets, policy predicates, exceptions and rollback criteria.

Observability & release evidence

Runtime traces, sandbox logs, artifact identity, decision journals and accountable approval.

Common objections

Objections are hypotheses until the sales record confirms them.

Will a blocking gate slow the team?

It can. The design question is whether the risk justifies the block and whether a traceable exception path exists.

Must we replace Jenkins, GitLab or GitHub Actions?

No. The policy is designed around the delivery chain in place unless migration is itself the decision.

Is human approval enough?

No single control is assumed sufficient. Approval context, authority and runtime enforcement are designed together.

What budget does this avatar have?

TO_VALIDATE. No individual income, departmental budget or price threshold is inferred from the role title.
