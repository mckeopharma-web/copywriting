Frustrations & fears

What this role is trying to escape.

Authority nobody can bound

Agent permissions, memory, connectors or reachable services are implicit, fragmented or wider than the production decision can defend.

Velocity that destabilises the platform

New models and tools arrive faster than reliability, rollback, incident ownership and evaluation criteria mature.

Cross-stack coordination as the bottleneck

AI platform, security, data, SRE, product, research and finance each hold a piece of the production boundary.

Late control review

Security, privacy, architecture or compliance asks arrive after implementation choices have already hardened.

Evidence rebuilt after the fact

When a release, incident or executive review happens, the team must reconstruct what the agent saw, called and changed.

Accountability without a defendable line

The role owns the production decision but cannot state the residual risk, stop condition or handoff in one reproducible record.

Wants & aspirations

What the role wants to add.

Bounded, observable runtime

Named ownership, explicit authority, tool boundaries, governed context and traces that survive the demo.

Production velocity without hidden instability

Promote new capabilities against acceptance and rollback criteria instead of confidence-by-demo.

Reusable decision evidence

Security, architecture, operations and leadership can inspect the same versioned execution evidence instead of asking for a new narrative.

Governed memory and data reach

Know what context persists, where it came from, who can revoke it and what a tool is allowed to receive.

Platform leverage

Give research and product teams reusable capabilities rather than one-off agent stacks that duplicate controls.

Defendable go / no-go decisions

Make residual risk, acceptance, escalation and shutdown explicit enough to defend across the decision unit.

Before & after state

The transformation is larger than “having an agent”.

Before

Experimental agents, fragmented architecture, implicit permissions, scattered evaluations and execution evidence. The role is accountable for a production boundary that is difficult to reconstruct and defend.

After

A bounded runtime with a named owner, authority contract, governed context, versioned evaluation, observable traces and reusable execution evidence. Residual risk and rollback conditions stay explicit.
